# Global Electrical Retail Analytics

An end-to-end data analytics project using **SQL and Microsoft Excel** to analyze sales, customers, products, and store performance.

## 📌 Project Overview

The goal of this project is to turn retail sales data into useful business insights.

The analysis covers:

- Sales and profitability
- Customer behavior
- Product performance
- Store performance
- Country and regional performance
- Revenue and profit trends

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **SQL** – Data analysis and business queries
- **Microsoft Excel** – Interactive dashboards and visualization
- **Power Query** – Data preparation
- **PivotTables & PivotCharts** – Analysis and reporting
- **Data Model & DAX** – Dynamic KPIs
- **Slicers** – Interactive filtering

## 📊 SQL Analysis

The SQL analysis focuses on questions such as:

- What are the total revenue and profit?
- Which countries generate the most revenue?
- Which products and categories perform best?
- Who are the highest-value customers?
- Which stores generate the most revenue and profit?
- How does revenue compare with profit?

SQL techniques used include:

- Aggregations
- Joins
- GROUP BY
- CASE statements
- Subqueries
- CTEs
- Window functions
- Date-based analysis

## 📈 Excel Dashboard

The Excel dashboard is divided into four sections:

### Sales Analysis
- Revenue, cost and profit KPIs
- Monthly revenue trend
- Revenue by country
- Revenue by product category
- Top 10 customers
- Profit by category
- Revenue vs profit

### Customer Analysis
- Total customers
- Customer revenue and profit
- Revenue per customer
- Repeat customer rate
- Top customers
- Customer purchase frequency
- Revenue by gender and country

### Product Analysis
- Total products
- Product revenue and profit
- Average revenue per product
- Top 10 products
- Revenue and profit by category
- Units sold

### Store Analysis
- Total stores
- Store revenue and profit
- Average revenue per store
- Top 10 stores
- Revenue and profit by country
- Revenue by state

## 🎛️ Interactive Dashboard

The dashboards include **Year slicers** that dynamically update KPIs, charts, and analysis, allowing performance to be compared across different years.
